Pros and cons

According to our mobile phone expert

  • Compact size: you can easily use this small 6.1-inch device with 1 hand.

  • Smooth with standard apps: you can quickly use standard apps for music, social media, or email without slowdowns.

  • Versatile cameras: take sharp photos with the standard lens and the wide-angle lens, even in low light.

  • Fewer details on a small screen: on this small screen, you’ll see fewer details in movies and games.

  • No sharp zoomed-in photos: zoomed-in photos aren't sharp, because you don't have a telephoto lens.

Description

You can easily use the Apple iPhone 15 128GB Black with 1 hand. This iPhone has a 6.1-inch screen size. That means it fits easily in your pocket. The A16 Bionic chip works well for music, email, and social media. Heavy apps sometimes lag, and you don't get AI features. You can take sharp photos with the wide-angle lens, even in low light. You can also photograph large groups and landscapes. With 128GB of storage, it fills up fairly quickly. That means you'll need to delete files more often.
